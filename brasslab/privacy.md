# Brass Lab Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-24

This privacy policy applies to the Brass Lab app for mobile devices (the
"Application"), made by Bit Decay Games, LLC (the "Service Provider").

## The short version

Brass Lab does not collect, transmit or share any information about you.
It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking,
and it makes no network connections. Everything you enter stays on your
device unless you choose to send it somewhere yourself.

## Information stored on your device

The records you keep in the Application (loads, components, costs, batches,
range sessions, chronograph data, notes) and any photos or chronograph files
you add are stored only in the Application's private storage on your device.
The Service Provider never receives them and has no way to see them.

## Camera and photos

The Application asks to use your camera or photo library only when you
choose to add a photo, such as a target or a receipt. Photos you add are
copied into the Application's private storage and go nowhere else.

## Files you share

- **Backups.** When you export a backup from Settings, the Application
  creates a file and hands it to your device's share or save screen. Where
  it goes from there is up to you. Backup files are not encrypted, so keep
  them somewhere you trust.
- **Sharing a recipe.** Sharing a load as text sends it only to the app you
  pick.
- **Opening files.** Chronograph files reach the Application only when you
  open or share one into it.

## Android Auto Backup

On Android you may turn on "Automatic Android backup" in Settings. It is off
by default. When it is on, Android's own backup service stores a copy of the
Application's data in your Google account. That copy is handled by Google
under Google's privacy policy; the Service Provider cannot access it. You can
turn it off at any time.

## Third parties

The Application contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking
code, and the Service Provider does not sell or share any information about
you, because it holds none.

## Your rights, including California (CCPA/CPRA)

Since the Service Provider collects no personal information, it holds none
to access, correct, delete, sell or share. Your data is under your control:
uninstalling the Application or clearing its storage deletes it from your
device. If you have questions about your rights, contact the Service
Provider at the address below.

## Children

The Application is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no
information from anyone, including children.

## Security

Your data is protected by your device's own security, such as its lock
screen and app sandboxing. Keeping your device and any exported backups
secure is in your hands.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with
a new effective date. If the Application ever begins collecting information,
this policy will be updated before that happens.

## Contact

Questions about this policy or about privacy in Brass Lab:
